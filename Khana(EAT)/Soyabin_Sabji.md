### Step Process vy Proces

- 1. Soyabean ko soft karo:Soyabean ko 10-12 minute garam paani mein bheego. Phir paani nikaal do aur haath se thoda press karke extra paani nikal do.
- 2. Tel garam karo:  Kadhai mein 2 tbsp tel garam karo medium flame pe.
- 3. Pyaz bhuno: Pehle pyaz daalo aur halka golden hone tak bhuno (2-3 minute).
- 4. Masale + adrak-lahsun daalo : Ab adrak-lahsun,Tamatar. green chilli, haldi, lal mirch, dhaniya powder daalo. 30-40 second bhuno (masala jalne na paaye).
  Thoda sa (2 chammach) paani daal kar 2 minute bhunein jab tak masala tel na chhod de. (Agar tamatar nahi hai toh masale daal kar sirf 30 second bhunein

- 5. Aloo daalo: Aloo daal do. 1-2 minute acche se mix karke bhuno.
- 6. Soyabean daalo: Ab soft kiya hua soyabean daal do. Acche se mix karo taaki masale chipak jaaye. Sabko masale ke saath 2 minute tak tez aanch par achhe se mix karke bhunein.
-  7. Paani aur Seeti (5–7 min): Tari (gravy) wali sabzi ke liye: 1 se 1.5 glass paani daalein.
      - Sukhi/latpati sabzi ke liye: ½ glass paani daalein. Upar se garam masala chhidkein, cooker ka dhakkan band karein aur medium flame par 2 seeti (whistles) aane dein.
       - Cover karke medium-low flame pe 8-10 minute pakao.  Beech-beech mein check karte raho. Aloo soft hone chahiye.
