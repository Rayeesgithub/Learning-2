### 1. Network / IT Support Engineer (CCNA)
- Gulf mein helpdesk, network aur IT support roles kaafi hote hain, aur fresher entry bhi possible hai.
- CCNA certification international level par maani jaati hai.
- Ye IT ke andar hi hai, to baad mein cloud ya security mein switch kar sakte ho.

### 2.  Supply Chain, Procurement & Logistics Management
- Gulf countries (Dubai, Saudi, Oman) trade, shipping, aur global logistics ke bohot bade hubs hain. India mein bhi e-commerce aur supply chain (Amazon, Delhivery, Reliance, Flipkart) ka bohot bada network hai.
-  India Demand: Flipkart, Amazon, Tata, Reliance, aur har manufacturing hub mein high demand.
- Gulf Demand: Dubai Ports, Saudi logistics mega-projects.
- Key Skills/Tools: Advanced Excel, SQL, aur Power BI ya Tableau (data visualization).

### 3. HSE (Health, Safety, and Environment) Officer / Engineer
- Engineering graduates ke liye yeh sabse safe aur transition-friendly career hai. India mein ab labor aur safety laws bohot strict ho rahe hain (real estate, metro construction, manufacturing mein), aur Gulf mein toh bina Safety Officer ke ek eent bhi nahi hili ja sakti.
- India Demand: Large construction firms (L&T, Tata Projects), manufacturing plants, aur metro infrastructure.
- Gulf Demand: Oil & Gas fields (ARAMCO, ADNOC), mega-cities (NEOM, Saudi Arabia).
- Mandatory Step: Aapko NEBOSH IGC ya  IOSH ka 1-2 mahine ka certification course karna hoga. B.Tech degree + NEBOSH ka combination bohot strong mana jata hai.

### 4.  Business Analytics & Operations Specialist
- Aapne B.Tech CS kiya hai, iska matlab aapka data aur logic strong hai. Agar coding job nahi milti, toh aap Non-Tech Data Roles mein ja sakte hain. Yahan aapko code nahi likhna hota, balki business data ko analyze karke management ko reports deni hoti hain.
-  India Demand: Har MNC (Deloitte, EY, KPMG, Genpact) aur startups mein massive hiring.
-  Gulf Demand: Multinational banks, real estate conglomerates, aur retail groups.
-  Key Tools: Advanced Excel, Power BI / Tableau, aur basic SQL.
-  India aur Gulf dono mein business operations aur reporting ka use hota hai. Aapki CSE knowledge bhi waste nahi hogi

### 5. Technical Procurement & B2B Technical Sales
- Engineering projects mein components aur machinery khareedne aur bechne ke liye technical knowledge ki zaroorat hoti hai. Ek CS graduate as a Technical Buyer ya B2B Sales Executive bohot jaldi grow kar sakta hai kyunki aap clients ke requirements aur technical aspects ko aaram se samajh sakte hain.
- India/Gulf Demand: Engineering firms, tech hardware distributors, aur software-as-a-service (SaaS) providers.
- Key Requirement: Good communication skills aur relationship building
- Income mein incentive milta hai, to salary achhi ho sakti hai.

### 6. Digital Marketing (SEO, Meta/Google Ads)
- India aur Gulf dono mein demand hai, kyunki har business ko online customers chahiye.
- Tumhe website aur analytics samajhne ka advantage hai, jo normal marketers ke paas kam hota hai.
- Freelancing se turant income bhi start ho sakti hai.
